# Roadmap

Rule: if a merchant has to touch code, the CLI or `.env` to do something, it's a product bug.
`.env` keeps infrastructure only (database URL, app secret); everything else lives in the admin.

The roadmap lives at **[shopnex.ai/roadmap](https://shopnex.ai/roadmap)**: what is shipped, what we're building now, and what comes next, with no dates promised.

Its source is the public [ShopNex Roadmap project](https://github.com/orgs/shopnex-ai/projects/8) on GitHub. The site rebuilds from it every hour. To suggest something, [open an issue](https://github.com/shopnex-ai/shopnex/issues) to request a feature.

Test plans merchants can see are in [e2e-plan](./e2e-plan.md).
