title: Reviews
description: Ask buyers for product reviews after fulfillment, approve them in the admin and show them on the storefront with the bundled Reviews app.

Reviews is a bundled app. After an order is fulfilled it emails the buyer a private link, holds what they write until you approve it, and shows approved reviews on the product page. Every review that comes from a link is marked **Verified buyer**, because the link only exists for a real order.

## Turn it on

The app runs as its own process next to ShopNex. In local development it listens on port `5100`.

- Set `REVIEWS_APP_URL` to the app's public origin (for example `https://reviews.example.com`) and `REVIEWS_APP_KEY` to its private key (`node scripts/app-keygen.mjs` makes one; keep it, a new key is a new app).
- Under Docker, start the `reviews` profile: `docker compose --profile reviews up`. The reviews live in a schema of their own (`app_reviews`) in the shop's Postgres, through `APP_DATABASE_URL` (the compose files set it), so they are backed up with the shop and the container needs no volume.
- With `create-shopnex`: `npx create-shopnex@latest my-shop --with reviews`.
- To skip it in local development, run `REVIEWS=none pnpm dev`.

Install it from **Admin → Apps**. It asks to read products and orders, and to send email to your customers from your shop's address (only to people who are customers of your shop).

## The page

The app's page has the numbers at the top (the average rating, how many reviews gave each rating, reviews waiting for you, requests sent, how many were answered and the share of requests that got a review) and four sections under them:

- **Reviews.** What each buyer wrote, with **Publish**, **Hide**, **Reply** and **Delete**. Narrow the list by status (Waiting, Published, Hidden), rating, product or a search. When reviews are waiting, **Publish all waiting** approves them at once. **By product** shows which products buyers write about and how they rate them.
- **Requests.** Every review request: scheduled, sent or answered, with the buyer's email. **Send now** asks for a scheduled one today instead of on its day.
- **Widget.** The snippet for your product page.
- **Settings.** Publishing, days after fulfillment, and the request email.

## Set it up

1. **Email.** Review requests are sent from your shop's address through **Settings → Email**, the same setup as your order emails, so there is nothing to enter in the app. Until email is on, requests wait and the app tells you how many.
2. **Days after fulfillment.** How long to wait before asking (default 7, `0` asks right away).
3. **The request email.** Under **Settings → The request email** write your own subject and message. `{product}` and `{shop}` are filled in, a blank line starts a new paragraph, and the link to the review page is added under your text. A preview shows what buyers get; leave the fields empty for the standard text.
4. **Publishing.** By default every review waits for you. You can let **4 and 5 star reviews** through automatically, or **every review**; the rest still wait for you.
5. **The widget.** Copy the snippet from the app into a **Custom HTML** section of your product template:

   ```html
   <div
     data-shopnex-reviews
     data-instance="https://shop.example.com"
     data-shop="default"
     data-product="{{ product.id }}"
   ></div>
   <script src="https://reviews.example.com/widget.js" async></script>
   ```

   Add `data-name="{{ product.title }}"` to name the product in the rating markup that search engines read.

## How it works

1. An order is fulfilled. ShopNex sends `order.fulfilled` to the app, which makes one review request per product in the order.
2. After the delay, the app emails the buyer from your address with a link to a plain review page. The link works once.
3. The review waits under **Reviews → Waiting** (unless auto-publish lets it through). **Publish** shows it on the storefront; **Hide** keeps it out. Published and hidden reviews stay listed, so you can change your mind, and **Delete** removes one for good.
4. **Reply to this review** adds a public answer that shows under the review in your store. Saving it empty removes it.
5. The widget shows the product's average, how many reviews gave each rating, and the reviews ten at a time (**Load more reviews**). It also adds the `aggregateRating` markup search engines use for star ratings in results.

## Limits and rate limiting

The public routes (the widget's `/public/reviews` and the review link `/r/…`) are rate limited per visitor, per minute: 120 widget calls and 20 link requests. Past that they answer `429` with a `Retry-After` header. The signed ShopNex webhook is not limited.

The app tells visitors apart by their socket address. Behind a proxy that is the proxy, so every visitor would share one limit: set `CLIENT_IP_HEADER` on the app to the header your proxy fills with the visitor's address (`cf-connecting-ip` on Cloudflare, `x-forwarded-for` on most others). The Dokploy file does this for you. Counts are kept in memory, so run one instance of the app.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Turn on email to send review requests":** set up sending in **Settings → Email**. Requests that are due go out within a minute.
- **Buyers get no email but the app shows no warning:** check the recent emails in Settings → Notifications and your SMTP provider. The app only sees "sent" or "email is off".
- **"Review requests are not set up":** the app could not subscribe to `order.fulfilled`. Check that ShopNex can reach `REVIEWS_APP_URL` (`REVIEWS_APP_INTERNAL_URL` if its public address is not reachable from the server).
- **The widget shows nothing or "No reviews yet":** `data-product` must be the product's id, and the review must be published.
- **Everyone gets "Too many requests":** set `CLIENT_IP_HEADER`, see above.

See the source in `apps/reviews` if you are building your own app.
