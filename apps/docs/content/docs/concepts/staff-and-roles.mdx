Accounts are invitation-only. The setup wizard creates the first account; everyone else needs a pending invitation. This covers email and OAuth sign-up.

- Invite staff from **Admin → Staff**. Invite links are copied from the admin and expire after <Value name="inviteExpiryDays" /> days.
- A **super admin** (platform role `superadmin`) can act in every shop without membership and manage users. The setup wizard's account is one.

## Roles

Each staff member has one role in a shop.

<Roles />

## Creating or recovering a super admin

```sh
pnpm create-super-admin --email you@example.com [--password ...]
```

A random password is printed if you omit `--password`.
