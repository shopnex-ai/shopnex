Self-hosting means your shop runs on a server you own, so you keep your data and pay no platform fees. You only pay the hosting provider, usually around $5 to $30 a month. You need a server with at least 2 GB of memory.

## How it works

1. **Pick a provider** below and click its button.
2. **Sign in or create an account** with that provider and confirm the deploy. It builds your shop and database for you.
3. **Open your shop's address** in a browser. The setup wizard creates your shop and your admin account.

<Callout type="warn">
  Complete the setup wizard at `/admin/setup` right after you deploy. Until you do, anyone who finds the address could claim your shop.
</Callout>

## After your shop is live

**Use your own domain.** Point your domain's DNS at the server, then re-run the installer with it. HTTPS is set up automatically.

<code>{`curl -fsSL ${SITE_URL}/install.sh | SHOPNEX_DOMAIN=shop.example.com sh`}</code>

## For developers

- **Admin, API and checkout** (`/admin`, `/api/*`, `/checkout/*`, plus admin assets) go to the admin app.
- **Everything else** (`/`, `/products`, `/collections`, `/pages`, theme assets) goes to the storefront.

Every option above runs this same image, fronted by Caddy on a single port. A platform only needs to forward that port and give the app a Postgres `DATABASE_URL`. To run the end-to-end suite, see [End-to-end tests](/docs/self-hosting/testing).
